I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science at the University of Virginia, advised by John Darrell Van Horn. My research focuses on biologically inspired neural networks. My current project, the *Digital Organoid*, models the timing of signals between neurons in a 3D, organoid-like network for image classification.

Before UVA, I earned B.Sc. degrees in Data Science and Neuroscience from the College of William & Mary.

I also teach at UVA's School of Data Science. In Spring 2026 I was the instructor of record for Foundations of Machine Learning (DS 3001).
